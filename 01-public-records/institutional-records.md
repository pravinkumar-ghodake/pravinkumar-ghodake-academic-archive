### 🔹 ISTAM Young Scientist Award Session — Selected Presenter

**Awarding Body:** Indian Society of Theoretical and Applied Mechanics (ISTAM)  
**Event:** 66th ISTAM Congress, IIT Kharagpur (2021)  
**Paper Title:** *Harmonic Scattering of S0 Lamb Wave: A Computational Study*  
**Session Type:** "Paper for the Young Scientist Award" — Award Session

**Recognition:**

Selected as one of the competitive entries in the ISTAM Young Scientist Award Session. The paper presented numerical simulations of nonlinear elastic inclusions in thin plates, detailing backscattered higher harmonics under one-way two-wave mixing, and drew key physics analogies between harmonically scattered elastic guided waves and 'Raman Scattering' in nonlinear optics.

**Official Reference:**  
🔗 [istam.iitkgp.ac.in/resources/2021/proceedings/Award_session/Abstract/SM/138abstract.pdf](https://istam.iitkgp.ac.in/resources/2021/proceedings/Award_session/Abstract/SM/138abstract.pdf)

### 🔹 Field-Weighted Citation Impact (FWCI) 1.51 — ICSV 2021

**Paper:** *Wave Propagation in a Media with Asymmetric Rate-Independent Hysteretic Nonlinearity*  
**Venue:** 27th International Congress on Sound and Vibration (ICSV 2021)  
**FWCI:** **1.51** (significantly above the global average of 1.0)  
**Database:** Elsevier Scopus

**Significance:**

An FWCI of 1.51 means the paper is cited 51% more than expected for its field and age. This is a strong indicator of research impact. The paper also marked a **major milestone** in nonlinear elastodynamics by breaking a decades-old academic consensus: it demonstrated for the first time that asymmetric contact interfaces activate a continuous spectrum of both odd and even higher harmonics (2f, 3f, 4f, ...), contradicting prior theoretical models (1994–2020).

**Reference:**  
🔗 [scopus.com/pages/publications/85117472334](https://www.scopus.com/pages/publications/85117472334?origin=resultslist)
