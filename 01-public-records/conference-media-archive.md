**Venue:** IIT Madras

**Description:**

### Unit Cell of Three Nonlinear Layers for the Design of Tunable Nonlinear Metamaterials

**Conference:** 184th Meeting of the Acoustical Society of America (ASA) · Acoustic Metamaterials Section  
**Year:** 2021  
**Presenter:** Pravinkumar Ghodake, Research Scholar, Department of Mechanical Engineering, IIT Bombay

**Description:**

<div align="justify">

This academic research portfolio entry features a technical conference presentation accepted and presented within the Acoustic Metamaterials Section of the 184th Meeting of the Acoustical Society of America (ASA). Bridging advanced solid mechanics, nonlinear ultrasonics, and wave propagation, the talk presents a novel unit cell configuration utilizing three nonlinear layers of equal widths to act as a tunable nonlinear metamaterial.

By varying the central layer intensity and total unit cell width, the study demonstrates precise control over the amplitudes of harmonically back-scattered and forward-scattered waves (2f), drawing direct parallels between acoustic harmonic scattering and Raman scattering in nonlinear optics.

</div>

**Viewers can explore:**

- Nonlinear ultrasonics and harmonic scattering
- Motivations and problem formulation
- Systematic parametric results across widths from 300 to 2100 microns
- Final conclusions

This media asset documents peer-reviewed academic dissemination and core theoretical and computational contributions in structural metamaterial design.

🎥 **Watch:** [Unit Cell of Three Nonlinear Layers for the Design of Tunable Nonlinear Metamaterials](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_BKjFGHdBps)

### Shape Optimization for the Design of Linear and Nonlinear Metamaterials | NDE 2021

**Conference:** NDE 2021 Virtual Conference & Exhibition · Indian Society for NDT (ISNDT)  
**Publication:** e-Journal of Nondestructive Testing (eJNDT Vol. 27(4))  
**Year:** 2021  
**Presenter:** Pravinkumar Ghodake, Research Scholar, Department of Mechanical Engineering, IIT Bombay

**Description:**

<div align="justify">

This academic research portfolio entry features a technical conference presentation delivered by Pravinkumar Ghodake at the NDE 2021 Virtual Conference & Exhibition organized by the Indian Society for NDT (ISNDT) and published in the e-Journal of Nondestructive Testing (eJNDT Vol. 27(4)).

Addressing a critical challenge in nonlinear ultrasonic testing — wherein system-generated higher harmonics from instrumentation, clamping forces, and couplants mask material-damage responses — the talk presents an automated shape optimization framework. By integrating MATLAB and COMSOL for finite element analysis, non-gradient algorithms are deployed to optimize layer thicknesses of steel-glass periodic structures to suppress a wide range of higher harmonics (2f, 3f, 4f, ...).

</div>

**Viewers can explore:**

- Introduction to nonlinear ultrasonic testing and parasitic system harmonics
- Motivation and masking effects from instrumentation and couplants
- Phononic bandgap design principles
- Shape optimization problem formulation
- Numerical implementation coupling MATLAB and COMSOL
- Harmonic suppression results and optimal layer thicknesses
- Final conclusions regarding applicability in NDE

This media asset documents peer-reviewed academic dissemination and core computational methodologies in advanced structural metamaterial and non-destructive evaluation design.

🎥 **Watch:** [Shape Optimization for the Design of Linear and Nonlinear Metamaterials | NDE 2021](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PyJpewnetOQ)
