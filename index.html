About My Research

I am a PhD Scholar in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay. While formally affiliated with IIT Bombay, I operate with the ethos of an independent researcher. My work is driven by a deep, self-directed curiosity to bridge fundamental theoretical mechanics with applied computational frameworks.

My primary research spans nonlinear ultrasonics, mechanical metamaterials, and inverse design. I specialize in developing numerical frameworks (FEM, spring-mass chains) to understand complex wave propagation, harmonic scattering, and early-stage damage detection. I am particularly fascinated by cross-disciplinary physics—such as drawing analogies between acoustic wave scattering and optical Raman scattering to engineer "elastically invisible" metamaterials.

As an independent researcher, I prioritize open, machine-readable science. Through this archive and my Google Site, I aim to provide a fully transparent, globally accessible record of my publications, conference presentations, and continuous research journey.
