# Academic & Intellectual Knowledge Base

## Pravinkumar Ramchandra Ghodake

This knowledge base is maintained by **Pravinkumar Ramchandra Ghodake**. Some academic and professional records use the shorter form **Pravinkumar Ghodake**; both forms refer to the same profile, identified by [ORCID 0000-0002-6755-4003](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-6755-4003).

The knowledge base is the structured information system accompanying the [Academic & Research Archive](https://pravinkumar-ghodake.github.io/pravinkumar-ghodake-academic-archive/). It brings together profile information, research and intellectual topics, source links, provenance records, reconciliation history, and database documentation.

The public-records section presents identifiable academic and professional records. This knowledge base documents their structured organization and the source relationships maintained in the canonical database.

## Research and intellectual scope

The research topics represented in the archive include nonlinear mechanics, elastic-wave physics, nonlinear ultrasonics, mechanical and phononic metamaterials, wave propagation, structural health monitoring, nondestructive evaluation, computational and experimental mechanics, inverse problems, optimization, and data-driven methods.

The wider intellectual and creative scope includes literature, multilingual creative writing, independent literary publishing, research communication, digital publishing, software projects, and essays on intellectual sovereignty, open science, authorship, and provenance.

The detailed research vocabulary is maintained in the [ORCID keyword and research index](https://github.com/pravinkumar-ghodake/pravinkumar-ghodake-academic-archive/blob/main/02-ai-knowledge-base/ORCID_KEYWORDS.md).

## Canonical database

The canonical database published in this directory is [`profile.sqlite`](https://github.com/pravinkumar-ghodake/pravinkumar-ghodake-academic-archive/blob/main/02-ai-knowledge-base/profile.sqlite). It uses **schema version 1.1**.

The database and its documentation are maintained together so that the records, structure, source relationships, and documented updates can be read in context.
