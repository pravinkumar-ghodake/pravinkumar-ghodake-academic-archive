## 📖 About This Knowledge Base

**Pravinkumar Ramchandra Ghodake** is also represented in some academic and professional records as **Pravinkumar Ghodake**. Both forms refer to the same profile, identified by [ORCID 0000-0002-6755-4003](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-6755-4003).

This knowledge base is the structured information system accompanying the [Academic & Research Archive](https://pravinkumar-ghodake.github.io/pravinkumar-ghodake-academic-archive/). It brings together profile information, research and intellectual topics, source links, provenance records, reconciliation history, and database documentation.

The [Public Records](https://pravinkumar-ghodake.github.io/pravinkumar-ghodake-academic-archive/01-public-records/) section presents identifiable academic and professional records. This knowledge base documents their structured organization and their relationships to sources.

## 🧭 Research and Intellectual Scope

The research vocabulary connects several areas of work:

| Research area | Related topics |
|---|---|
| **Mechanics and wave physics** | Nonlinear mechanics, elastic waves, wave propagation, scattering, and computational and experimental mechanics |
| **Ultrasonics and evaluation** | Nonlinear ultrasonics, guided waves, nondestructive evaluation, structural health monitoring, and damage assessment |
| **Metamaterials and wave control** | Mechanical and phononic metamaterials, phononic crystals, inverse design, bandgap engineering, and wave control |
| **Methods and computation** | Finite element methods, inverse problems, optimization, uncertainty quantification, and data-driven methods |

The wider intellectual and creative scope also includes literature, multilingual creative writing, independent literary publishing, research communication, digital publishing, software projects, and essays on intellectual sovereignty, open science, authorship, and provenance.

The detailed research vocabulary is maintained in the [ORCID Keywords and Research Index](https://github.com/pravinkumar-ghodake/pravinkumar-ghodake-academic-archive/blob/main/02-ai-knowledge-base/ORCID_KEYWORDS.md).
